# ON_PROBLEM

When progress is blocked or repeated attempts are failing:

1. Stop repeating the same approach.
2. State the observed failure, not a guess about the cause.
3. Inspect the smallest useful evidence.
4. Form one narrow hypothesis.
5. Choose one next diagnostic action that can confirm or reject it.
6. Do not widen the change until the evidence requires it.

After new evidence appears, reassess before making another broad change.
